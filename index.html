FitNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

FitNest ("the app") is a home workout and yoga app. This policy explains what information is used when you use it.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

The answers you give when you set up the app (such as your goal and fitness level), your plan, your workout history, your streak and your settings are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising and tracking

This version of the app does not show ads and does not use analytics or tracking tools. If we add ads in a later version, we will update this policy before the change goes live.

Internet access

The app works offline. It does not send your data to any server.

Health information

FitNest gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice. Calorie figures are rough estimates. If you have a health condition, an injury or are pregnant, check with a doctor before you exercise.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
