GK Nest: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

GK Nest ("the app") is a General Knowledge quiz app. This policy explains what happens to your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts and no sign-up. If you type your name or choose an exam, these choices are saved only on your phone so the app can greet you and show the right questions. They never leave your device.

Information stored on your phone

Your language, chosen exam, quiz progress, coins, streak and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. This data never leaves your phone. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted.

Internet, ads and analytics

This version of the app works offline. It does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking tools, and does not connect to third-party services.

Children

The app is intended for students aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Disclaimer

GK Nest is an independent practice app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any government body, exam conducting body or Public Service Commission.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features such as ads or online rankings, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the developer through the email address listed on the app's Google Play page.
