Zombie Dash: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Zombie Dash ("the app") is an endless runner game. This policy explains what happens to your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The game has no accounts and no sign-up.

Information stored on your phone

Your coins, upgrades, best wave and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. This data never leaves your phone. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted.

Internet, ads and analytics

This version of the game works offline. It does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking tools, and does not connect to third-party services.

Children

The app is intended for players aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Disclaimer

Zombie Dash is an independent game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any government body, game company, brand or organisation.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features such as ads or online rankings, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the developer through the email address listed on the app's Google Play page.
